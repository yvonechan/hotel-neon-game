# Hotel NEON — art direction and prompts

Mode: built-in image generation, not API/CLI fallback. Two generated assets are copied into `public/art/` and referenced by the interface. Original outputs were preserved. No generated image is used as a precise data chart or an authoritative source of tiny technical labels.

## Direction

Original Japanese anime mystery comedy for adult engineering teams. Cozy mountain/onsen hotel, midnight blue and warm amber, restrained cyan/coral accents, expressive adult cast and a tired-worker robot. Draw inspiration from the genre rather than recreating any existing franchise characters. No horror, gore, weapons or real-brand identities.

PIP: compact cream service robot, dark oval display with cyan eyes, hotel bowtie, low wheeled base and folded towels. Mio: adult events director, chestnut bob, teal suit, cream blouse, amber scarf and staff lanyard. Future Mio illustrations must match the retained original; her expression should look like normal work, not announce guilt.
